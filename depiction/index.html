CCLinker

Swipe a Control Center module to jump to its settings.

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Cellular, Hotspot, AirDrop and VPN open their Settings page.

Focus and Low Power Mode open their Settings page.

The music widget opens the app that is playing, or an app of your choice.

Pick the gesture (a swipe in any direction, or hold) and turn individual links on or off in Settings → CCLinker. Changes apply without a respring.
